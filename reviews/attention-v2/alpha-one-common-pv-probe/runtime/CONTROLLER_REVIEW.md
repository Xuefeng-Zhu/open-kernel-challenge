## Completed negative results are not execution errors

A full native completion requires exact source/support/diagnostic/frozen-worker
hashes, correctness+diagnostic completion, status complete/exit0, exact counts
and two uninstrumented timing families. Its benefit flag may be false: failed
per-fixture timing acceptance is a valid completed negative result. Controller
success means complete/closed execution, not that performance improved.

If **either** scored fixture has zero fast opportunity, the valid negative
path requires opportunity=false consistent with two nonnegative integer counts,
at least one zero, no timing families, timing_complete=false, zero measured-

benefit.admitted=false. All-positive counts claiming no opportunity, invented

frequency negative.
