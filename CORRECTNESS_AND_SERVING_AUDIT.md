## Confirmed ownership issue, qualified coverage

In `reviews/attention-v2/mixed_fa3_fused_full_small.py`, `_TICKETS` at lines 57–65 is device-global, used by each fallback at line 256. Partials at lines 248–253 are invocation-local. The atomic election at lines 196–219 can combine completions from separate calls into one counter despite distinct caches. With two splits, completion order A0,B0,A1,B1 can make B merge before B1 exists, then never elect A. No weak-memory or acquire/release failure is needed.

Four scored paths retain this code: sliding decode B1/B16/B32 and full decode B32. Twelve prerequisite cases also reach E2; SPLITS=1 bypasses election. The official TASK/AGENTS/reference/checks describe fresh inputs and sequential alternating CUDA graph replays, not an explicit concurrent-call requirement. This is an upstream robustness defect, not evidence invalidating passed official sequential cases.

Those full verify cases now dispatch to FA3; do not transfer their coverage to current fallback decode or new fused paths.
